How the value is fitted — read this before trusting a rank

1 · Size curve — fitted, not assumed

2 · No-token clock — launch odds by age

3 · Farm verdict — netted to break-even

4 · Census — all 758 protocols accounted for

Method: launch value from a log–log size curve fitted on the snapshot's traded tokens, times a bounded 7-day growth prior, times the no-token clock's launch odds. Farm plans are costed per protocol (capital by TVL, hold by age, gas + 5% APR opportunity cost) and netted to a FARM / THIN / SKIP verdict at a 5% farmer allocation. No network, no names hardcoded — run main.ts on a new snapshot and the whole board re-ranks itself.
